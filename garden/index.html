A SMALL WORLD OF THINGS I LOVE

Home Renovation

As an architecture school dropout, I’ve always been interested in how spaces feel. When we finally had a house of our own, my chance has finally come. There’s always a corner of my home I’m imagining a little differently. Little by little, those thoughts turn into sketches, samples, weekends of work—and something I get to live with.

The kitchen

The kitchen became the biggest chapter so far. I took it on without a general contractor, coordinating trades and discovering, with some amusement, how much it resembled my day job - Dependencies, timelines, usability, aesthetics, budgets, tradeoffs—only this time, I had paint on my clothes.

Now there’s the smell of garlic in the air, dinner simmering on the stove, and me getting distracted by how lovely the kitchen looks in the evening light.

The side yard

The side yard started as a muddy stretch of ground we mostly hurried past. We designed and built it ourselves, working out the curves, laying the stepping stones, and moving what felt like an unreasonable amount of gravel.

Now it’s a small garden path tucked between the house and the trees. The stone lantern at the end gives me a reason to wander out there, even when I have absolutely nowhere to go.

We are in the process of renovating our powder room. Stay tuned on how it turns out!
