Ramble

Talk through a thought, get it back as tidy notes.

A Mac menu-bar app I designed and built. Tap fn, talk, tap fn again, and Ramble types a clean note wherever your cursor is.

  • On-device speech
  • No API key needed
  • macOS 26

Try it

Pick something to say, then tap fn (or press Space). This runs as a simulation in your browser; the real app listens to your voice on your Mac.

Between one fn and the next

  1. 01 · Listen

    On-device speech

    Apple's newer speech engine, the one behind Notes and Voice Memos. It works offline after a one-time model download.

  2. 02 · Clean up

    Fixed rules first

    Fillers, stutters and stray "you know"s go before any AI step. It's instant, and it can't invent anything.

  3. 03 · Tidy

    Formatted by content

    Apple Intelligence on device, or Claude Haiku with your own key. Steps become numbered lists; emails keep their sign-off.

  4. 04 · Type

    Right where you are

    Pasted at your cursor with real headings and bullets, and left on your clipboard too.

Built for how people actually talk

Self-corrections

Say "let's meet Tuesday, no wait, Wednesday" and only Wednesday is kept. Rewrites are only accepted if they add no new words.

Tuesday, no wait, Wednesday

Spoken structure

"First… second… third…" becomes a numbered list. Say "new paragraph" or "bullet point" to format directly.

first → 1.  second → 2.

Accent training

Read a short passage aloud and Ramble learns how your accent tends to be transcribed, with passages focused on the sounds most often misheard from Cantonese and Mandarin speakers.

wery very · tree three

Private by default

Speech and tidying both run on your Mac. Nothing leaves it unless you choose Claude as the tidying engine.

no account · no key · offline

Meet the cat

A pixel-art cat sits on the bar while you talk and reacts like a listener: ears up while you speak, a head tilt when you pause, yarn when you go quiet too long.

    Under the hood

    Swift and SwiftUI, compiled with a small build script
    Speech
    Apple SpeechAnalyzer, with the classic recognizer as a fallback
    Tidying
    Apple Intelligence (Foundation Models), or Claude Haiku
    Requires
    macOS 26 on Apple Silicon, Apple Intelligence on
