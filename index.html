Charlene Yang

Senior Product Designer

I do my best work at the messy start of a project, when the problem is still fuzzy. My job is turning ambiguity into direction: framing the problem, naming the trade-offs, and getting everyone looking at the same picture.

I think in systems. In data products a filter is never just a filter, so I design the patterns and rules that hold up across the whole platform.

I partner with engineering from day one, and lately I've been prototyping with AI to get working ideas in front of people faster.
